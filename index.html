Questions
Before you reach out

    

      
Which marketplaces do you work on?
Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Shopify and TikTok Shop, for sellers in the USA, UK, Australia and Canada. I also have experience with Temu and OnBuy.

      
Can you get a suspended account reinstated?
I prepare the appeal: reading the notice, finding the root cause, gathering documents and writing the plan of action. Reinstatement depends on the reason and the marketplace's decision, so I cannot promise an outcome. I will tell you plainly what the chances look like after reviewing the case.

      
Do you help with company registration and account creation?
Yes. I support LLC creation for US sellers, LTD creation for UK sellers, and seller account creation on the main marketplaces.

      
Can I hire you for one task?
Yes. Product research, a listing clean-up, an appeal or a store setup can be one-off projects. Daily account management is ongoing.

      
How do we get started?
Send an inquiry with your marketplace, your goals and what is not working. I reply with a few questions or a suggested next step. You can also message me on WhatsApp.
